Running the LC-PolScope with QPSC

These steps take you from a switched-off microscope to retardance and orientation images in QuPath. If a message or an image does not look like the description here, see If you see this, check this.

1. Turn on2. Start the software3. Load and focus4. Calibrate5. Background6. Acquire7. Results8. Next slide9. Shut down

What you do for each slide

For every slide: focus, calibrate on empty glass, collect a background, then acquire. Calibration and background both measure the slide itself, so they are repeated for each new slide.

  1. Hamamatsu camera, left port
  2. Oryx camera, right port
  3. Stage controller and joystick
  4. Liquid crystal controller
  5. Stage
  6. Eyepieces
  7. Port positions and PORT dial
  8. Condenser
  9. Lamp power supply
  10. Slider above the condenser

1. Turn on the microscope

  1. Switch on the lamp (label 9). The green light comes on. Leave the brightness knob where it is.
  2. Switch on the stage controller (label 3). The switch is on its back panel.
  3. Press POWER - STATUS on the liquid crystal controller (label 4).
  4. Switch on the Hamamatsu camera (label 1). The switch is on its back, and the STATUS light comes on.
  5. Turn the nosepiece to the objective you will use.
  6. Turn the PORT dial to 5 (label 7) to send the light to the camera. Position 1 sends it to the eyepieces.
Photos: where each switch is

2. Start the software

Start the three programs in this order.

  1. Open Micro-Manager and load MM2-Flash4-20261021.cfg.
  2. Double-click start_server.bat, and wait for Ready for connections... Leave that window open.
  3. Open QuPath.
  4. Open Extensions > QuPath SCope > Utilities > Microscope Configuration > Communication Settings... and click Connect Now. The status reads Status: Primary connected (auxiliary will connect on use).
  5. Click OK.

A message that will not go away? See starting up and connecting.

3. Load the slide and focus

  1. Place the slide on the stage, coverslip down, with the label on your right.
  2. Open Extensions > QuPath SCope > Acquisition controls > Live Viewer.
  3. Click Live: OFF so it reads Live: ON.
  4. Open Stage Control > Navigate, move to your sample, and focus.

Live image very dark, or the stage will not move? See the live image.

4. Calibrate

Calibration tunes the liquid crystals for this slide. Run it on empty glass close to the area you will image.

  1. In the Live Viewer, move to a clear, empty area near your sample.
  2. Open Extensions > QuPath SCope > LC-PolScope > LC-PolScope Setup...
  3. Click Calibrate Liquid Crystals..., leave the settings as they are, and click OK.
  4. Wait a few minutes without touching the microscope. Cancel does not stop the calibration, so let it finish.
  5. Check that the header of LC Calibration Complete says good.
  6. Click Apply to acquisition, then Apply if a second window asks.
  7. Click Collect background now... and go on to step 5.

Result not good, or an error instead of a result? See calibration problems. Imaging something much brighter than tissue, such as the test target? See choosing the exposure before you calibrate.

5. Collect the background

The background is a picture of empty glass that is subtracted from every tile. Take it right after calibrating, on the same slide.

  1. Stay on the empty area and turn the focus 10 to 20 um away from sharp focus.
  2. Click OK in the prompt. The Background Collection dialog opens.
  3. Check that Objective: and Background profile: match your setup, then click Acquire Backgrounds.
  4. Wait for Background Collection Complete, then focus on your sample again.

Asked for a background profile, or not sure which one to use? See background profiles.

6. Acquire

  1. Open Extensions > QuPath SCope > Acquisition controls > Bounded Acquisition.
  2. Type a Sample Name:.
  3. Check that Objective: matches the nosepiece.
  4. Under ACQUISITION REGION, move the stage to the center of your region, click Use Current Position as Center, and type Width (um): and Height (um):.
  5. Open MODALITY-SPECIFIC OPTIONS and read the Ready to acquire? box. No line should start with Fix.
  6. Click Start Acquisition.
  7. In the LC-PolScope: this slide reminder, click Acquire.

Stay off the bench while it runs. To stop early, click Cancel Acquisition; the current tile finishes first.

A Fix or Check line in the box? See Ready to acquire? lines.

Using an overview image instead of stage coordinates

If the slide has an overview image with annotations in the project, open that image and use Extensions > QuPath SCope > Acquisition controls > Acquire from Existing Image. It adds steps that line up the overview with the stage.

7. Look at the results

Stitching runs on its own after the run. The Stitching queue window shows progress, and the finished images appear in the project list.

Image name containsWhat it shows
_retardance_Retardance. Divide the pixel value by 100 to get nm.
_orientation_Slow-axis angle. Divide by 100 to get degrees.
_orientation_color_Color picture of both, for viewing only
Only the sample name and a numberThe five raw frames
  1. Open the retardance image and look at empty glass. It should be close to black, with no grid across the tiles.

Grid, stripes or odd colors? See image problems.

Changing the colors of an image

Open the retardance or orientation image, choose Extensions > QuPath SCope > Utilities > Project Tools > Orientation Colour View..., pick a Colour map: and Full brightness at:, and click Show on viewer. Stored values do not change. Export colour image... saves the result as a new image.

8. Next slide

  1. Load the new slide and focus (step 3).
  2. Calibrate on the new slide (step 4).
  3. Collect a background on the new slide (step 5).
  4. Acquire (step 6).

9. Shut down

  1. Save the project and close QuPath.
  2. Click in the server window and press Ctrl+C.
  3. Close Micro-Manager.
  4. Switch off the lamp, then remove your slide. A lamp left on a slide dims the tissue under it.
